# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is for people who like their Mac to work the way they do.

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.
- Assume good faith. Bug reports from frustrated users are still gifts; reply in kind.
- Keep private things private: do not post other people's personal information, and redact your own (device serials, clipboard contents) in logs you share.
- Respect licenses and credit. Do not paste code you do not have the right to relicense under MIT (see CONTRIBUTING).

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use a private report through the repository's Security tab (choose "Report a vulnerability" and say it is a conduct report) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
